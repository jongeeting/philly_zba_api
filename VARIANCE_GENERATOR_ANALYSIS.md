# Variance Generator Analysis (2013-2025)

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 16,938 ZBA variance appeals from 2013-2025, compared against 81,617 by-right zoning permits.

**Key Finding:** Four reforms—allowing roof decks, eliminating parking minimums, expanding commercial uses, and allowing 4 stories—would eliminate **610 variance appeals per year** (46.8% of total caseload) and improve the by-right development rate from 82.8% to 90.1% (+7.3 points).

### Key Insights

1. **Roof Decks** are the #1 variance driver by far (24.4% of all appeals, 318/year)
2. **Commercial uses** drive 22.1% of appeals (287/year) - significant demand for neighborhood commercial
3. **Parking** drives 20.8% of appeals (271/year) - parking minimums force many variances
4. The "Big 3" (roof decks + commercial + parking) account for **67% of all variance appeals**
5. Height variances are surprisingly rare when explicitly stated (only 0.4% of appeals)

### Key Thresholds

- **Allow 2 units (duplex):** Resolves only 3.4% of unit variances
- **Allow 3 units (triplex):** Resolves 32.9% of unit variances
- **Allow 4 units (fourplex):** Resolves 45.9% of unit variances ✓
- **Allow 5 units:** Resolves 51.0% of unit variances
- **Allow 6 units (sixplex):** Resolves 58.0% of unit variances

**Recommendation:** Allow **fourplexes (4 units) by-right**
- Resolves nearly half of dwelling unit variances
- Aligns with pending state "missing middle" housing bills
- Traditional Philadelphia building type
- Good balance between density and neighborhood compatibility

**Alternative:** If state bills require duplexes only (2 units), this would resolve only 3.4% of unit variances—very limited impact.

## Policy Recommendations: Optimal Thresholds

Based on frequency analysis and impact modeling, here are the recommended policy thresholds:

### Priority 1: **Roof Decks** (HIGHEST IMPACT)
- **Current rule:** Roof decks require variances in most residential zones
- **Recommendation:** Allow roof decks by-right
- **Impact:** Eliminates ~318 variance appeals/year (24.4% of caseload)
- **Rationale:** Roof decks are common urban amenities with minimal neighbor impact

### Priority 2: **Parking Minimums**
- **Current rule:** Most zones require minimum parking spaces
- **Recommendation:** Eliminate parking minimums citywide
- **Impact:** Eliminates ~271 variance appeals/year (20.8% of caseload)
- **Rationale:** Philadelphia has strong transit/walkability; developers can build parking if market demands it

### Priority 3: **Commercial Uses**
- **Current rule:** Residential zones prohibit commercial uses
- **Recommendation:** Allow neighborhood commercial (corner stores, cafes, small offices) in residential zones
- **Impact:** Eliminates ~287 variance appeals/year (22.1% of caseload)
- **Rationale:** Traditional Philadelphia neighborhoods had corner stores; current zoning treats them as incompatible

### Priority 4: **Height Limits**
- **Current rule:** Many residential zones cap buildings at 35 feet (3 stories)
- **Recommendation:** Allow 4 stories (45-48 feet) by-right
- **Impact:** Eliminates ~5 explicit height variance appeals/year (0.4% of caseload)
- **Rationale:** Four-story buildings are compatible with urban neighborhoods and common in successful corridors

### Priority 5: **Dwelling Unit Density**
- **Current rule:** Single-family zoning and restrictive density limits
- **Recommendation:** Allow fourplexes (4 units) by-right
- **Impact:** Resolves 45.9% of dwelling unit variances (~29 appeals/year)
- **Rationale:** "Missing middle" housing provides naturally affordable options; aligns with state bills

## Reform Scenario Analysis

### Scenario: 4 Stories + No Parking + Roof Decks + Commercial

This reform package includes the four highest-impact changes:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Appeals completely eliminated** | 7,935 (46.8%) |
| **Appeals partially helped** | 928 (5.5%) |
| **Total appeals impacted** | 8,863 (52.3%) |
| **Reduction in ZBA caseload** | 610 appeals/year |
| **Current by-right rate** | 82.8% |
| **New by-right rate** | 90.1% |
| **Improvement** | +7.3 percentage points |

**Key Finding:** This combination eliminates nearly half of all variance appeals and brings Philadelphia to a 90%+ by-right development rate.

### What This Means

- **Current:** 1,303 variance appeals per year
- **After reforms:** ~693 variance appeals per year
- **Reduction:** 47% fewer variance appeals
- **Developer savings:** ~$3-9 million/year in variance costs (610 appeals × $5k-15k each)
- **Time savings:** 610 projects avoid 3-6 month ZBA delays
- **City savings:** Reduced ZBA processing burden, staff time, hearing costs

## Methodology

**Data Sources:**
- ZBA appeals: 16,938 appeals (2013-2025) from Philadelphia CARTO API
- Zoning permits: 81,617 permits (2013-2025) from Philadelphia CARTO API
- Total projects: 98,555

**Detection Method:**
- Keyword-based extraction from appeal text
- Regex patterns for height (stories/floors) and dwelling units
- Simple keyword matching for parking, roof decks, commercial, etc.

**Limitations:**
- Keyword detection may miss some appeals or misclassify
- Height and unit values extracted only when explicitly stated
- Some complex appeals may have issues not captured in appeal text
- "Partially helped" estimates are conservative

**Validation:**
- Results align with detailed Tier 2 analysis (within 5 percentage points)
- Total appeal counts match CARTO database
- Percentages calculated as share of both appeals and all projects
