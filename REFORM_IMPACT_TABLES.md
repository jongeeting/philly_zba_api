## Summary: What Would These Reforms Achieve?

### Tier 1 Reforms (4 Stories + Triplex + Parking)
**Conservative estimate**: Eliminate **130 appeals/year** (10% of total)
- Reduces current 1,303 appeals/year → 1,173 appeals/year
- Improves by-right rate from 83.2% → 84.7% (+1.5 points)

**Optimistic estimate**: Impact **294 appeals/year** (22.5% of total)
- Some of the "partially helped" might convert to by-right
- Could reach 85-86% by-right rate

### Tier 1 + Tier 2 Combined (+ Roof Decks + Sixplex + Commercial)
**Conservative estimate**: Eliminate **669 appeals/year** (51% of total)
- Reduces current 1,303 appeals/year → 634 appeals/year
- Improves by-right rate from 83.2% → **91.2%** (+8.0 points!)

**Optimistic estimate**: Impact **691 appeals/year** (53% of total)
- Could reach 92% by-right rate
- Only ~8% of projects would need variances

### Key Insight: Tier 2 is Critical
- **Tier 1 alone**: Modest impact (10% eliminated)
- **Tier 1 + Tier 2**: Transformative impact (51% eliminated)
- **Roof decks are the game-changer**: Account for most of Tier 2's impact

## Methodology Notes

**Data Source:** 16,938 ZBA appeals from 2013-2025 (post-reform period)

**Detection Method:** Keyword-based extraction from appeal descriptions
- Height: Regex pattern for "X stories" or "X floors"
- Dwelling units: Patterns for "X dwelling/family/units"
- Parking: Keyword "parking"
- Roof deck: Keywords "roof deck"
- Commercial: Keywords like "commercial," "retail," "office," etc.

**Classification:**
- **Completely Eliminated**: Appeals where reforms address ALL detected issues (single-issue)
- **Partially Helped**: Appeals where reforms address SOME but not all issues (multi-issue)
- **Total Impacted**: Complete + Partial

**Limitations:**
- Keyword detection may miss some appeals or misclassify
- "Partially helped" may or may not convert to by-right (depends on project)
- Conservative estimates focus on "completely eliminated" only
- Some appeals have no detectable issues in text (included in "Not Helped")

**Reform Definitions:**
- **Tier 1**: Height ≤4 stories, Units ≤3 (triplex), Parking elimination
- **Tier 2**: Adds Roof decks, Units ≤6 (sixplex), Commercial/mixed-use allowances
