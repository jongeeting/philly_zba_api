SUMMARY
====================================================================================================

Parking variances in no-minimum districts likely involve:

1. **Parking MAXIMUMS** - Districts may have maximums, not just minimums
   (e.g., RSA-5 might limit parking to prevent garage conversions)

2. **Parking LOCATION requirements** - Where parking must be located
   (e.g., rear yard only, not in front setback)

3. **Parking DESIGN standards** - Dimensions, surfacing, access
   (e.g., must be paved, must have certain dimensions, driveway width)

4. **Accessory structure rules** - Garages counting against lot coverage
   (e.g., detached garage exceeds accessory structure allowance)

5. **Historic or contextual requirements** - In certain overlays

This explains why 'eliminating parking minimums' alone won't solve all parking variances.
Need to also address parking maximums, location rules, and design standards.
