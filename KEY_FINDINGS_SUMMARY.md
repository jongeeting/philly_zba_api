# Philadelphia ZBA Variance Analysis: Key Findings Summary

**Analysis Period:** 2013-2025 (13 years)
**Total Appeals Analyzed:** 16,954
**Total Zoning Permits:** 81,617
**Date:** January 26, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Philadelphia's zoning code forces **1,303 variance appeals per year**, creating delays, costs, and uncertainty for housing development. Our comprehensive analysis reveals that:

1. **RSA-5 (rowhouse district) alone accounts for 41% of all variance appeals**
2. **Five reforms would eliminate 598 appeals/year** and achieve a 90% by-right rate
3. **Roof decks are the #1 variance driver** (24.4% of all appeals)
4. **Residential zones drive 67% of appeals** despite being "less intensive" zones

## RSA-5 Deep Dive: The Biggest Problem

**What is RSA-5?**
- Residential Single-Family Attached (rowhouse district)
- Philadelphia's most common residential zoning
- Typical rules: 35-foot height limit (3 stories), parking minimums, restrictive accessory structures

**Scale of the Problem:**
- **6,892 appeals over 13 years** (530/year average)
- **41.2% of ALL variance appeals** citywide
- **Top 5 districts = 76% of appeals**, with RSA-5 being over half of that

## Policy Recommendations

### Priority 1: Reform RSA-5 Rules
**Why:** 41% of all variance appeals, nearly all rowhouse development

**Specific changes needed:**
1. **Allow roof decks by-right** → eliminates ~120 RSA-5 appeals/year
2. **Eliminate parking minimums** → eliminates ~70 RSA-5 appeals/year
3. **Increase height to 45 feet (4 stories)** → eliminates ~15 RSA-5 appeals/year
4. **Allow duplexes/fourplexes** → eliminates ~30 RSA-5 appeals/year

**Total impact:** ~235 appeals/year eliminated from RSA-5 alone (44% reduction)

### Priority 2: Fix All Residential Districts (RSA-5, RSA-3, RM-1, RSD-3)
**Why:** 67% of all variance appeals

**Changes:**
- Same reforms as RSA-5 across all residential zones
- Particular focus on roof deck and parking restrictions

**Total impact:** ~400 appeals/year eliminated

### Priority 3: Liberalize CMX (Commercial Mixed-Use) Districts
**Why:** 25.6% of appeals, supposed to be "flexible" zones

**Changes:**
1. **Expand allowed commercial uses** → eliminates ~85 CMX appeals/year
2. **Allow roof decks by-right** → eliminates ~60 CMX appeals/year
3. **Eliminate parking minimums** → eliminates ~50 CMX appeals/year

**Total impact:** ~195 appeals/year eliminated from CMX districts

### Combined Impact
**Residential reforms + CMX reforms = ~595 appeals/year eliminated**

This matches our comprehensive package estimate of 598 appeals/year.

## Bottom Line

**The variance crisis is concentrated:**
- **41% in one district (RSA-5)**
- **67% in residential zones**
- **76% in just 5 districts**

**The solutions are clear:**
- **Allow roof decks** (24% of appeals)
- **Eliminate parking minimums** (21% of appeals)
- **Expand commercial uses** (22% of appeals)
- **Allow 4 stories** (7% of appeals)
- **Allow fourplexes** (helps 46% of dwelling unit variances)

**The impact would be transformative:**
- **598 fewer appeals/year** (46% reduction)
- **90% by-right rate** (up from 83%)
- **$3-9 million/year** saved in variance costs
- **Faster housing production** with less uncertainty

**Start with RSA-5:** Fixing this one district could eliminate ~240 appeals/year (18% of total citywide). It's the low-hanging fruit that would have the biggest impact.
