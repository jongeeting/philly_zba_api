| Period | Avg Appeals/Year | Avg Permits/Year | Avg Total | Variance Share | By-Right Share |

**Key Findings:**
- **2012 reform improved by-right rate by +2.7 percentage points** (79.7% → 82.4%)
- **Continued improvement through 2025**: 83.7% by-right (up +4.0 points from pre-reform)
- **Tax abatement uncertainty period (2020-2021)** showed highest by-right rate (83.4%) - likely simpler projects
- **Post-2022 period** stable at 83.1% by-right (slight decline from peak but still +3.4 points above pre-reform)

## Table 2: Variance Drivers by Time Period

Shows what percentage of ZBA appeals in each period were seeking relief from specific regulations.

| Variance Driver | Pre-reform (2007-2012) | Post-reform (2013-2018) | Recent (2019-2025) |
|-----------------|------------------------|-------------------------|--------------------|
| **Total Appeals** | **9,532** | **8,458** | **8,480** |
| Height (any) | 1,529 (16.0%) | 432 (5.1%) | 156 (1.8%) |
| Height ≤4 stories | 331 (3.5%) | 93 (1.1%) | 59 (0.7%) |
| Dwelling Units (any) | 115 (1.2%) | 280 (3.3%) | 562 (6.6%) |
| Duplex (2 units) | 8 (0.1%) | 8 (0.1%) | 14 (0.2%) |
| Triplex (3 units) | 6 (0.1%) | 99 (1.2%) | 149 (1.8%) |
| Parking | 2,006 (21.0%) | 1,919 (22.7%) | 1,606 (18.9%) |
| Roof Deck | 810 (8.5%) | 2,251 (26.6%) | 1,900 (22.4%) |

**Key Trends:**
- **Height variances declining**: From 16.0% pre-reform to just 1.8% recent (likely due to 2012 reform reducing height restrictions)
- **Parking remains consistent**: 19-23% across all periods (major driver)
- **Roof decks surging**: From 8.5% to 26.6% (became major issue post-reform)
- **Triplex variances increasing**: From 0.1% to 1.8% (more demand for middle housing)

**Tier 1 Reform Opportunities** (≤4 stories + triplex + parking):
- Would impact 18.9% + 1.8% + 0.7% = ~21-23% of recent appeals (overlap likely)
- Parking is the biggest single driver (18.9%)

**Tier 2 Reform Opportunities** (roof decks):
- Would impact 22.4% of recent appeals
- Has become the #1 dimensional variance driver

**Notes:**
- Percentages show share of total appeals in that period
- These are keyword-based estimates (actual numbers may vary)
- Categories overlap: one appeal may have multiple drivers
- Low dwelling unit detection suggests many appeals don't explicitly state unit counts
