## Key Trends

### 1. Appeal Volume Declining
- **2007-2012 average**: 1,493 appeals/year
- **2023-2025 average**: 1,037 appeals/year
- **Reduction**: 456 fewer appeals/year (31% decline)

### 2. By-Right Rate Improving
- **Pre-reform**: 74.4% by-right
- **Recent**: 80.6% by-right
- **Improvement**: +6.2 percentage points

### 3. 2012 Reform Impact Sustained
- Initial improvement: +5.0 points (2007-2012 → 2012-2017)
- Further improvement: +1.2 points (2012-2017 → 2023-2025)
- Total improvement: +6.2 points over 15+ years

### 4. Proposed Reforms Could Double Improvement
- Current: 80.6% by-right (19.4% need variances)
- Your reforms eliminate ~292 appeals/year
- Projected: ~88-90% by-right (10-12% need variances)
- **Additional improvement**: +8-10 percentage points

## Notes

1. **Variance Rate** = ZBA Appeals ÷ Total Zoning Permits
2. **By-Right Rate** = (Total Permits - ZBA Appeals) ÷ Total Permits
3. Actual permit numbers for each year not available through public API
4. By-right percentages from earlier analysis using internal data
5. 2026 is partial year (data through January)

## Interpretation

**Lower variance rate = Better zoning code**
- More projects can proceed without ZBA review
- Faster, cheaper development process
- Less uncertainty for developers
- Reduced ZBA workload

The improvement from 74.4% to 80.6% by-right means:
- About 6 out of every 100 projects that previously needed variances can now proceed by-right
- ~450 fewer variance appeals per year compared to pre-reform baseline
- Sustained improvement suggests reforms are working
