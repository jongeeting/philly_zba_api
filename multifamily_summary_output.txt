====================================================================================================
SUMMARY: MULTIFAMILY HOUSING BARRIERS
====================================================================================================

THE PROBLEM:

  1. Even multifamily zones (RM-1) require 160 variances/year
  2. Single-family zones (RSA-5) block conversions (530 appeals/year)
  3. Dwelling unit limits force 71 variances/year for density
  4. Parking minimums add 271 variances/year (many for multifamily)
  5. Height limits add 97 variances/year (many for 4+ story multifamily)

THE SOLUTION:

  1. Allow fourplexes by-right → eliminates 29 unit-related appeals/year
  2. Eliminate parking minimums → eliminates 271 appeals/year
  3. Allow 4 stories → eliminates 97 appeals/year
  4. Allow roof decks → eliminates 318 appeals/year

  COMBINED: ~598 appeals/year eliminated (46% of total)

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR MULTIFAMILY:

  • Small multifamily (2-4 units) is 'naturally affordable' housing
  • Doesn't require subsidies or government programs
  • Matches Philadelphia's historic building patterns (rowhouse conversions)
  • Current rules force most small multifamily through variance process
  • Costs $5,000-15,000 and 3-6 months per variance
  • Makes small-scale development economically marginal

THE ASK:

  1. Allow fourplexes by-right in RSA and RM zones
  2. Eliminate parking minimums (let market decide)
  3. Allow 4 stories in residential zones
  4. Allow roof decks by-right (common amenity)

  Result: 46% fewer variance appeals, faster multifamily production
